Selected projects.

Four different problems. The same curiosity behind each.

01APP DEVELOPMENT

ScrapWise

A scrap management platform that connects households, collectors and traders through pickup requests, locality prices, and collection workflows.

My contribution — Flutter Android/web interfaces, authentication, multilingual screens and role-based experiences. The team also works on scrap forecasting and e-waste analysis.

Flutter / Dart / UIRepository ↗
02APPLIED ML · RESEARCH

Hybrid-PCOS-Analysis

A research application exploring symptom-based assessment and ultrasound-image PCOM visibility through two separate machine-learning workflows.

Under the hood — Logistic Regression, MobileNetV2 and Grad-CAM image explanations. Designed for educational exploration, not medical diagnosis.

Python / TensorFlow / StreamlitRepository ↗
03FULL-STACK WEB

smart-bank-loan-prediction-system

A web application that lets users explore preliminary loan eligibility from their financial details and understand repayment estimates.

Under the hood — React, Flask, a machine-learning classifier and an EMI calculator. It illustrates the lending process; it is not a bank approval system.

React / Flask / Scikit-learnRepository ↗
04NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Smart Campus Lost and Found Matcher

A campus utility where students report lost and found items, then find possible matches even when the descriptions aren't identical.

Under the hood — TF-IDF and cosine similarity rank descriptions; SQLite keeps track of item reports and their status.

Python / Flask / NLPRepository ↗
